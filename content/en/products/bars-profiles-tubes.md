## Product options

- Flat, round, square and merchant bars
- Open and hollow profiles
- Welded and seamless steel tubes

## Typical supply range

- **Sizes:** Bars Ø6–200 mm · flats 20–300 mm · tubes/profiles 10–500 mm
- **Length:** 6–18 m
- **Typical grades:** S235JR · S275JR · S355JR · ASTM/SAE grades on request
- **Surface / finish:** Black, pickled, shot-blasted, primed or galvanized
- **Quality:** Prime, overrolling and secondary / non-prime, subject to stock
- **Applications:** Fabrication, steel structures, machinery, piping and general engineering

*Indicative ranges only. Final dimensions, grade, coating, quality and certificates are confirmed in the individual offer.*
