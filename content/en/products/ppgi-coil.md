PPGI coils are pre-painted galvanized steel products with a protective zinc coating and a painted surface finish.



They are widely used for roofing, wall cladding, sandwich panels, appliances, construction products, and decorative steel applications.



Mettaroll supplies PPGI coils in different colors, grades, thicknesses, widths, and quality levels depending on current stock availability, supplier offers, and customer requirements.



\## Specifications



\- \*\*Thickness:\*\* 0.3mm – 1.5mm

\- \*\*Width:\*\* 600mm – 1250mm

\- \*\*Coating:\*\* galvanized base with painted surface

\- \*\*Colors:\*\* standard and special colors upon availability

\- \*\*Standard:\*\* EN 10346 / EN 10169 upon supplier confirmation
